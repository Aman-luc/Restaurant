Our Story

A room built for the evening

Noir & Or opened with a single idea: candlelight, patience, and precise cooking say more than a long menu ever could. Every plate that leaves our pass has passed through the hands of two people at most — nothing more, nothing rushed.

Our kitchen sources from the same six farms year-round, and our cellar is built one grower at a time. What you taste is the result of restraint, not excess.
